# sporke

Archetype: fast. Rig: medium. Template: template:fast.

1. Role: the fast, goofy member of a four-reptile sewer ninja team. The role, never the original.
2. Name: Sporke, a misprint of spork. The sensei named each gecko after what he found in one dumpster bag.
3. Gag: he has never hit himself with his own nunchaku, and he says so before every fight. He votes anchovies.
4. Weapon: two plastic sporks tied with a shoelace. They never break. One spork is missing a tine.
5. Specials: Spork Flurry, a forward dash of four pokes and a knockdown swing. Directional variants come from the template.
6. Lines: `character.json` has the single lines and `lines.json` has the pools.
7. Team-up: Dog-Pile with the other three geckos. It waits for the wave 1 plan (D83, D87).
8. Tells: a paper soda-jerk hat instead of any mask, and the select-screen stat TINES: 3 OF 4.
9. Palette: `sporke` and `sporke-alt` in `content/palettes/palettes.json`, from `master.json`.

Stats: the fast band. The reference, Brat, is on the small rig, so the numbers are tuned against her band with the in-memory search from D86 (D87).
