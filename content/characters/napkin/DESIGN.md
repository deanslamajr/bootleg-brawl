# napkin

Archetype: grappler. Rig: medium. Template: template:grappler.

1. Role: the big quiet member of a four-reptile sewer ninja team. A long-reach powerhouse. The role, never the original.
2. Name: Napkin, the thing the sensei found folded in the dumpster bag. The sensei named each gecko after one item.
3. Gag: he folds himself into a corner when embarrassed, and he wants olives on everything.
4. Weapon: a mop held as a bo-staff. It never breaks. The mop head hides during the special, where a spinning handle takes over.
5. Specials: Wet Floor, a spinning mop sweep with a wide box. Directional variants come from the template.
6. Lines: `character.json` has the single lines and `lines.json` has the pools.
7. Team-up: Dog-Pile with the other three geckos. It waits for the wave 1 plan (D83, D88).
8. Tells: a paper napkin folded into a headband instead of any mask, and the select-screen stat REACH: YES.
9. Palette: `napkin` and `napkin-alt` in `content/palettes/palettes.json`, from `master.json`.

Stats: the grappler band. The reference, Boomer, is on the large rig, so the numbers are tuned against his band with the in-memory search from D86 (D88). The grappler band has `taken` 0, so only dps and clear time count.
