# strawe

Archetype: ranged. Rig: medium. Template: template:ranged.

1. Role: the ranged member of a four-reptile sewer ninja team. The role, never the original.
2. Name: Strawe, a misprint of straw. The sensei named each gecko after what he found in one dumpster bag.
3. Gag: he talks through the straw, so every threat comes out as a whistle. He wants extra sauce on everything.
4. Weapon: a bendy straw used as a blowgun, with the bend taped straight. It never breaks. Spitballs are projectiles in `content/items/items.json`.
5. Specials: Spit Take, two volleys of three spitballs in a fan. A held attack charges a big spitball through the charge-shot module, like Mensa.
6. Lines: `character.json` has the single lines and `lines.json` has the pools.
7. Team-up: Dog-Pile with the other three geckos. It waits for the wave 1 plan (D83, D87).
8. Tells: a paper straw wrapper over his eyes as a mask, with the holes in the wrong place, and the select-screen stat AMMO: SPIT.
9. Palette: `strawe` and `strawe-alt` in `content/palettes/palettes.json`, from `master.json`.

Stats: the ranged band. The reference, Mensa, is on the small rig, so the numbers are tuned against her band with the in-memory search from D86 (D87).
