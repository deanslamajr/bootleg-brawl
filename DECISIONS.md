# Decisions

Each row records a choice the plan did not settle, or a place where the build differs from the plan. Add a row for every new one.

| ID  | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D01 | Sprites are baked from vector overlay shapes on shared rig poses, not from Aseprite files or Flux concept art.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Neither Aseprite nor Flux is available to the build. Procedural overlays keep the plan's paper-doll model: one rig per size class, overlays per body part, a bake step and a per-actor atlas. If Aseprite sources appear, `export-sprites` still exports them.                                                                                                                      |
| D02 | The pose library lives in `packages/tools/src/rig/poses.ts`. `pnpm tools gen-rigs` writes `content/rigs/*.rig.json`. If the two differ, CI fails.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Hand-typing about 135 poses per rig as JSON is error-prone. The generator keeps the JSON regenerable from source.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| D03 | Atlases are indexed PNGs. The browser decodes them to palette indices and recolors them per palette on the CPU at load time, instead of a palette-swap fragment shader.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | It costs zero art per tier, the same as the shader. It works the same on WebGL and WebGPU and keeps the renderer simple.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| D04 | The renderer asks Pixi for WebGL first.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The game draws only sprites and text. WebGL is the most widely supported path, and WebGPU adds no benefit at this size.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D05 | Music is tracker JSON (`content/music/*.track.json`) synthesized at run time with Web Audio, instead of Furnace exports to OGG or Opus.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Furnace is not available. A tracker file is tiny and regenerable. Beat maps are derived at pack time, and gameplay beats come from the tick, never from audio.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D06 | Frames are trimmed in the atlas, and each frame stores its own anchor. The rig frame box applies to the idle pose.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Lying and lunging poses are wider than the plan's frame boxes. The validator rejects any idle pose that leaves its frame box.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D07 | A states file can `extend` a template and `patch` single fields. Characters extend the archetype templates instead of copying them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | It keeps four characters from carrying four copies of the base move set. Patches hold only the keys written in the file. The merged state is validated.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D08 | Boxes in `states.json` are in medium-rig unit space. The compiler scales them by the actor's rig and body scale.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | One template fits every rig size. On write, the sandbox box editor converts to unit space.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| D09 | Hurtboxes default to `"auto"`, which the compiler derives from the pose's joints.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Every frame then has a hurtbox without hand authoring. Explicit boxes override it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| D10 | For two grounded fighters, hit resolution compares only x and lane. If either one is in the air, height also counts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Small enemies such as sewer rats were otherwise immune to punches. Jumping over attacks still works.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| D11 | A player hit four times within 70 ticks is knocked down. The get-up invulnerability lets them escape.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Without it, a swarm stun-locked a player to death.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| D12 | An enemy at zero health cannot be hit, and an airborne target takes at most three juggle hits.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | A falling knocked-out enemy was juggled forever.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| D13 | After an enemy walks into view, it stays on screen while the camera is locked. Knife throwers walk into range before they throw.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | A thrower held a scroll lock from off screen.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D14 | Scroll locks take `hold: false` for walk-on waves that spawn without stopping the camera.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | It cut idle scrolling under the plan's 15% target without longer fights.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| D15 | A segment ends at 140 px before its last pixel, not at the edge.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Walking to the very edge added idle time with nothing to fight.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| D16 | The `training-room` stage exists for the sandbox, balance runs, character goldens and perf. It is not in the campaign.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Those tools need a stage with no locks. GAME SELECT: ALL lists it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| D17 | Archetype bands in `content/balance/bands.json` were recorded from the v1 characters with `pnpm tools balance --record`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | The plan names the envelope but not its numbers. Each v1 archetype has one character, so v1 sets the center that later characters must match.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D18 | Boss health scales by 35% for each extra player.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Four players otherwise end a boss before its second phase.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| D19 | Golden replays cover each character in the training room, each campaign stage with two bots, and one four-player session. The compiled content hash covers scales, frame data and stages, so a failed replay names a content change.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | It follows the plan's golden set, and a failure explains itself.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| D20 | The scripted bot plays golden replays, metrics, balance and the Playwright test. It reads only sim state and the tick.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Bot input is deterministic, so a bot run is a scripted input log.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| D21 | Feature flags come from `content/settings.json` (packed into the core bundle) and from `?flags=`. A leading `-` turns a module off.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | The plan names both sources.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| D22 | All bundle data loads at boot. Atlases, layers, portraits and sounds load lazily. Bundles go through the Cache API.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Def indices must be stable for determinism, so the compiler needs every def. The data is about 300 KB. The heavy assets stay lazy as the plan asks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| D23 | The build writes `.gz` and `.br` files next to text assets. The nginx image serves `.gz` with `gzip_static`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The plan asks for both. The default image has no Brotli module, but a Brotli-capable image can use the `.br` files with no rebuild.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| D24 | The deploy host defaults to `bootleg.llmgag.world` (`BOOTLEG_HOST` overrides it). The manifests attach an HTTPRoute to the shared Gateway, like Marble Melancholy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | The plan asks for a new subdomain on the same cluster. Marble Melancholy uses a Gateway route, not an Ingress.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D25 | On a hit, a thrown knife disappears instead of dropping a knife pickup. Sarge's hair still catches them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | The floor filled with knives.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D26 | Bystanders have 999 HP. A hit costs the attacker 200 points. A grabber can grab a bystander, and the rescue drops food.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | It matches the plan's civilian rules without letting players KO the neighbors.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D27 | The repository is public at github.com/deanslamajr/bootleg-brawl.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | The plan was delivered as a folder without git. CI and the deploy script expect a repository, and nothing in it needs to be private.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| D32 | When the tick goes backwards, a bot resets its cooldowns.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | A bot kept across stages in the app held absolute tick counters and stopped attacking after a stage change. Fresh bots never see it, so recordings are unchanged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| D34 | Losing window focus or hiding the tab pauses a stage in play mode.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | A missed alt-tab cost a life.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D37 | The validator rejects post-v1 fields that no module reads: `rules.widescreen`, `rules.timerHealth` and `rules.invertGravity` when set, a non-empty `forks` list, segment kinds `fork`, `qte` and `cutscene`, and stage types other than `walk-right`, `elevator`, `auto-scroll` and `arena`. A rule key that no schema declares fails too. The schema keeps the fields. A module that declares the rule in its stage schema fragment, or registers a segment controller for the kind, makes it legal. | A field that parses and does nothing is a content lie. The schema stays so that a future module can read the field without a migration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D45 | The core carries a few module-specific fields as accepted debt: the `tiny` component, `COND.charged`, `F.camFly` and `F.emerging`. Modules never replace a core system or reuse a core system or component name, and `tests/unit/modules.test.ts` asserts it. A stage with civilian placements must list `civilians` in `rules.requires`.                                                                                                                                                             | Moving the fields out needs a component-per-module refactor that the review did not ask for. Without the module, bystanders act as ordinary enemies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| D46 | The browser frame gate measures main-thread frame work, from the start of the loop callback to the end of Pixi's draw, and sim tick time. At 4x CPU throttling, p95 frame work must be under 12 ms and p95 tick under 4 ms. The nightly perf project also gates first playable under 5 s at 20 Mbps with 40 ms latency, and JS heap under 300 MB at the Game 0 boss with Game 1 prefetched.                                                                                                           | The old gate measured the vsync interval, which stays near 16.7 ms whatever the frame costs. The plan's load and memory targets had no test. The heap figure covers the JS heap only, not GPU textures. On 2026-10-17 local Chromium measured frame work and tick of 2.6 and 0.8 ms at 4x, 4.4 and 1.3 ms at 8x and 8.0 and 2.2 ms at 16x, first playable 1365 ms and heap 11.5 MB. |
| D47 | If `SMOKE_URL` is unset, the Playwright smoke project does not exist. A run that names the smoke project fails with a message that names `SMOKE_URL`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | The smoke test passed against the local preview server, which is not what a smoke test is for.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D48 | An ESLint block forbids writes and mutating calls on the sim from `apps/game`, the renderer, audio and input: assignments, updates, `delete` and array mutators on `sim.c`, `sim.g` and the other state fields, and calls such as `enterState` and `spawnActor`. `sim.step` stays legal. The sandbox is exempt because it is a tool that edits a paused sim on purpose. The rule is a tripwire, not a proof, because an alias such as `const c = sim.c` escapes it.                                   | Invariant 2 was held by convention only. The two-runner golden job remains the proof.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| D49 | The validator checks each idle pose against its anchor. The lowest ink row must be within 1 px of the anchor row, and the middle of the feet (the lowest three ink rows) within 4 px of the anchor column. Pack rejects a baked sheet whose sides are not powers of two up to 1024.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Plan line 408 asks for the anchor at the foot point and power-of-two sheets. The remediation plan's stricter draft, no ink below the anchor row and the whole ink centered within 2 px, failed five v1 actors. The sewer rat's outline sits 1 px below its anchor, and bellies, weapons and the scooter pull the ink center off the feet.                                           |
